**Client:** Waikato Regional Council

Limnotrack was commissioned by Waikato Regional Council to assess the vulnerability of lakes across the Waikato region to climate change. A total of 85 priority waterbodies were identified for investigation, spanning shallow floodplain lakes, humic peat lakes, dune lakes and man-made reservoirs used for water supply, irrigation and flood control.

For each waterbody, we developed timeseries of water balances, configured and calibrated an ensemble of process-based hydrodynamic models (GLM, GOTM, DYRESM), and ran climate change scenario simulations to project changes in lake thermal structure, evaporation and water level. The project establishes a framework for ongoing lake modelling that can evolve alongside future monitoring, calibration and catchment modelling work, and is documented through this report alongside a companion interactive web application for interrogating and visualising the results.

Under warming air temperatures, the models project corresponding increases in surface water temperature and evaporation rates, and decreases in lake level, with the magnitude of change varying by lake classification.
