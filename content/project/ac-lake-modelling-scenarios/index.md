**Client:** Auckland Council

Building on the [Healthy Waters lake monitoring platform](../healthy-waters/), this phase 2 project delivers calibrated, process-based water quality models for ten Auckland and Northland lakes — Rototoa, Tomarata, Wainamu, Spectacle, Te Kanae, Pehiakura Small, Pupuke, Kereta, Kuwakatai and Slipper — using the AEME R package to drive the GLM-AED hydrodynamic–ecological model.

Each lake is calibrated through a staged PEST++ iterative ensemble smoother (iES) workflow that progressively fixes water balance, temperature, suspended solids, organic matter/oxygen, nutrients and phytoplankton dynamics, with explicit performance gates (e.g. temperature KGE ≥ 0.6, oxygen KGE ≥ 0.3) at each stage. Calibrated models then underpin downstream scenario work, including an ensemble-for-prediction approach for lakes that feed into uncertainty analysis further down the catchment.

The calibrated model suite will be used to run climate change and nutrient reduction scenarios, giving Auckland Council a consistent, process-based basis for comparing management options across the region's priority lakes.
