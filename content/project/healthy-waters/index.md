**Client:** Auckland Council (Healthy Waters)

Limnotrack developed an R Shiny application for Auckland Council's Healthy Waters team to support ongoing monitoring and modelling of lakes across the Auckland region, including Lake Tomarata, Spectacle Lake, Lake Wainamu, Lake Rototoa and others. Built on the LakeEnsemblR/LERNZmp platform, the app brings together climate data, in-lake sensor and monitoring data, water quality observations, and an ensemble of hydrodynamic lake models (FLake, GLM, GOTM, Simstrat, MyLake) in a single interface.

The platform allows Healthy Waters staff to review model calibration and performance for each lake, explore water temperature and water quality time series at depth, and compare simulated conditions against observations, supporting evidence-based lake management decisions across the region.
